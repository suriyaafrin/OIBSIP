Keep it alive

Care basics, no green thumb required

Ask us anything
☀️

Light

Most houseplants want bright, indirect light — near a window, not directly on the sill. Rotate the pot weekly so growth doesn't lean.

💧

Water

Check the top inch of soil before watering, not the calendar. Overwatering causes far more plant deaths than underwatering.

🌡️

Climate

Keep plants away from cold draughts and radiators alike. Most tropicals are happiest between 18–27°C, same as most people.
